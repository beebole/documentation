<Info>
  The Jira integration can't be connected yet. On **Settings** \> **Integrations** \> **Jira**, Beebole shows a coming-soon message instead of the **Connect to Jira** button. To share your requirements, contact [support@beebole.com](mailto:support@beebole.com) and Beebole lets you know as soon as the integration is ready. Accounts already connected to Jira keep working as described on this page.
</Info>

Beebole's Jira integration imports your Jira Cloud projects and issues into Beebole so your team can track time against their Jira work. For accounts already connected, new issues created in Jira and issues renamed in Jira are reflected in Beebole automatically, and a manual sync brings in the rest of the structure.

The integration imports your Jira structure into Beebole's **Projects and activities**, for time tracking with costs, billing, and expenses, or into **Planning and tasks**, for resource assignment.

| Jira | Projects and activities | Planning and tasks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project | Project | Task |
| Issue | Subproject | Sub-task |

---

## What syncs and what stays in Beebole

| What syncs from Jira | What stays in Beebole |
| --- | --- |
| **Projects:** Imported as Beebole projects or tasks. | **Approval workflows:** Beebole handles the timesheet lifecycle. |
| **Issues:** Imported as Beebole subprojects or sub-tasks. | **Billing rates:** Managed exclusively in Beebole. |
| **Users:** Jira users are mapped as Beebole people. | **Budgets:** Set within Beebole's billing and budget settings. |

---

## What's included

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Jira projects and issues">
    Active projects and issues at the time the integration was enabled are created in Beebole, in a category named **Jira**. While the integration is enabled, an issue created in Jira is added to Beebole automatically, and an issue renamed in Jira is renamed in Beebole. New Jira projects, renamed projects, and projects archived or restored in Jira are brought in line when you click **Manual sync**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Jira users">
    All active users in your Jira site are created as people in Beebole when the integration is first enabled. They receive the role chosen in **Default role for imported employees**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Items managed in Beebole">
    Timesheet entries, approval workflows, billing rates, budgets, and expenses remain in Beebole and are not affected by the integration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Managing an existing connection

If your Beebole account is already connected to Jira, go to **Settings** \> **Integrations** \> **Jira**. Your connection appears under the coming-soon message, with these settings:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Jira account connected** | Shows the Jira account the integration uses. |
| **Default role for imported employees** | The role given to Jira users imported as people. You can review roles in **Settings** \> **Person Roles**. |
| **Where to import your tasks** | **Projects and activities** (project time tracking with rates and billing) or **Planning and tasks** (resource planning and assignment). |
| **Enable integration** | Turns syncing on or off. |
| **Manual sync** | Shown under **Manually sync Jira projects and issues** while the integration is enabled. It compares your Jira site with Beebole and shows how many items were **Created**, **Archived**, **Unarchived**, **Deleted**, and **Renamed**. |

**Default role for imported employees**, **Where to import your tasks**, and **Reset connection** can only be changed while the integration is turned off.

To track time on the imported items, the **Jira** category must be part of the timesheet. Go to **Settings** \> **Account Settings**, open **Timesheet and Planning Settings**, and on the **Categories** tab add the Jira category under **Record time on these project categories** (or **Record time on these plannings** if you import into **Planning and tasks**). See [Timesheet settings](/help/documentation/timesheetSettings#categories).

## Privacy: people removed in Jira

Beebole periodically checks the Jira accounts it imported. When a person's Jira account is closed, Beebole anonymizes the matching imported person automatically — their name and email are scrubbed — while their time records stay intact for reporting. This keeps the account aligned with Atlassian's privacy requirements without any manual cleanup.

## Disabling the integration

<Warning>
  If you disable the integration, changes made in Jira no longer sync to Beebole. Previously imported data remains in Beebole as local records. If you click **Reset connection**, you can't connect again until the integration becomes available, since the **Connect to Jira** button is not shown for now.
</Warning>

To disable the integration, go to **Settings** \> **Integrations** \> **Jira** and turn **Enable integration** off. You can turn it back on at any time to resume syncing.

To disconnect your Jira account entirely, turn the integration off, then click **Reset connection**.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect Beebole to Jira today?">
    Not yet. The Beebole Jira integration is coming soon, and the Jira page under **Settings** \> **Integrations** shows a coming-soon message instead of **Connect to Jira**. Contact [support@beebole.com](mailto:support@beebole.com) to share your requirements, and Beebole lets you know as soon as it is ready. Accounts already connected to Jira keep working.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often does Beebole sync with Jira?">
    For connected accounts, issues created or renamed in Jira are reflected in Beebole automatically, as Jira reports the change. Other changes, such as new Jira projects or projects archived in Jira, come into Beebole when you click **Manual sync** on the Jira integration page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I choose which Jira projects to import?">
    No. The Beebole Jira integration imports all active projects and issues from your Jira site. You can organize and filter imported items within Beebole after the import.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the integration work with Jira Server or Data Center?">
    The Beebole integration is designed for Jira Cloud. If you use Jira Server or Data Center, contact [support@beebole.com](mailto:support@beebole.com) to discuss options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to imported data if I disable the integration?">
    All previously imported projects, issues, and people remain in Beebole as local records. They are not deleted. Only future changes from Jira stop syncing until you turn the integration back on.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
