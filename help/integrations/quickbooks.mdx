Beebole's QuickBooks Online integration works in two directions. When you connect it, Beebole imports your QuickBooks structure — customers, items, and employees — so both systems share the same customers, items, and employees. From then on, you can export time entries to QuickBooks as time activities in one click. This simplifies invoicing, payroll, and financial reporting for your organization.

---

## How it works

When you enable the integration, Beebole imports your existing QuickBooks Online data: **Customers** and **Items** are created as projects in Beebole, and QuickBooks **employees** are created as Beebole people — assigned the default role you select during setup, as with the other integrations.

To export, you simply select a period: Beebole takes all time entries for that period and automatically creates the corresponding **time activities** in QuickBooks. After each export, Beebole shows an **Entries successfully exported** count, plus an expandable **Entries not exported** list if any entry failed — you can also verify the created time activities in QuickBooks.

<Info>
  Only time entries are exported to QuickBooks. Expenses are not synced — they are handled in Beebole only.
</Info>

## Setting up the QuickBooks integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integration settings">
    Go to **Settings** \> **Integrations** in your Beebole account, then select **QuickBooks** from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to QuickBooks">
    Click **Connect to QuickBooks**. A popup appears asking you to sign in to your QuickBooks Online account; follow the Intuit prompts to authorize Beebole. The popup closes automatically when the connection is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the default role">
    Select the **Default role for imported employees** to assign to the people imported from QuickBooks, as with the other integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable integration">
    Toggle **Enable integration** on. This triggers the import: Beebole brings in your QuickBooks customers, items, and employees. The toggle is what starts the sync — the connection alone does not import anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the connection">
    Once enabled, the QuickBooks integration shows as active in your integration settings, and the imported customers, items, and employees appear in Beebole. You can now export time entries to QuickBooks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exporting data to QuickBooks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the QuickBooks integration">
    Go to **Settings** \> **Integrations** and select **QuickBooks** from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a period and export">
    Under **Select period to export**, choose the period you want to export and click **Export** — that's the only input needed. Before sending anything, Beebole syncs your QuickBooks structure (customers, items, and employees) so that entries are matched against up-to-date data. It then fetches the time entries for that period and automatically creates the corresponding time activities in QuickBooks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Beebole shows an **Entries successfully exported** count, and an expandable **Entries not exported** list if any entry failed. To confirm the export, you can also open QuickBooks and verify the time activities were created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If the structure sync that runs before the export reports errors, the export does not start. Beebole shows **Export stopped: QuickBooks sync had errors** with the hint **Resolve the sync errors below, then try exporting again**, and lists the failing items under **Entries not synced**. Fix those items, then click **Export** again.
</Info>

<Info>
  Only one export runs at a time. Click **Export** again while one is still going — a double-click, or a second browser tab — and Beebole refuses the second run with **An export to QuickBooks is already in progress. Please wait for it to finish before starting another.**, so the same entries are never sent twice.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Export after the approval cycle is complete for a given period, so the hours sent to QuickBooks are final.
</Tip>

## What data is synced

| Direction | Data | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QuickBooks → Beebole (at setup, then in real time and on each sync) | **Customers** and **Items** | Created as projects in Beebole; later additions and renames in QuickBooks are reflected automatically |
| QuickBooks → Beebole (at setup, then on each sync) | **Employees** | Matched to an existing Beebole person by email address, or created as a new person with the **Default role for imported employees**. Employees without an email address in QuickBooks are skipped |
| Beebole → QuickBooks (on export) | **Time entries** | Created as time activities in QuickBooks, carrying the entry's billing rate, billable or non-billable status, and its comment as the activity's description. Durations are sent as whole hours and minutes, so each activity lands on the exact time tracked rather than a rounded decimal |

<Info>
  Because Beebole imports the QuickBooks customers, items, and employees at setup and refreshes them on every sync, exported time activities stay linked to the right customers, items, and employees automatically — even when the matching customer or item sits at a different level of your Beebole project hierarchy. Expenses are not synced in either direction — they are handled in Beebole only.
</Info>

## Keeping customers, items, and employees in sync

While the integration is enabled, customers and items created or renamed in QuickBooks are reflected in Beebole automatically. To pull the latest changes on demand, go to **Settings** \> **Integrations** \> **QuickBooks** and click **Manual sync** under **Manually sync QuickBooks customers and items**. The same sync also runs automatically each time you click **Export**, right before the time entries are sent.

Each sync also imports employees added in QuickBooks since the integration was enabled. For every new QuickBooks employee with an email address, Beebole looks for an existing person with that same email and links the two. If no one matches, Beebole creates a new person with the employee's name and email and assigns the **Default role for imported employees** you selected during setup. Employees without an email address in QuickBooks are not imported, and if no default role is set, the sync reports **No default role configured to import new employees** and skips the employee import.

After a sync, Beebole shows the counts of items **Created**, **Archived**, **Unarchived**, **Deleted**, and **Renamed**. Anything that could not be synced appears under **Entries not synced**, for example a **Failed to sync employee** message naming the person who could not be created.

---

## Disconnecting QuickBooks

To disconnect the QuickBooks integration, go to **Settings** \> **Integrations** \> **QuickBooks**, toggle **Enable integration** off, then click **Reset connection**. The **Reset connection** button is disabled while the integration is enabled, so you must turn the toggle off first. Previously exported data remains in QuickBooks — disconnecting only removes the ability to send new exports.

Disconnecting from the QuickBooks side works too: if you remove Beebole from your apps in QuickBooks, Beebole is notified and closes the connection cleanly — the integration shows as disconnected the next time you look at it in Beebole.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the QuickBooks integration real-time?">
    Customers and services are synced automatically and kept up to date in real time. Employees added in QuickBooks are imported when a sync runs — when you click **Manual sync**, or automatically before each export — not in real time. Time activities are exported manually — you choose the period and trigger the export when ready, giving you full control over what gets sent to QuickBooks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export the same date range twice?">
    No. Beebole blocks any export that starts on or before the last day you already exported, so you cannot resend an overlapping period. If you try, Beebole shows the error "You can only export entries from the last day of the period already exported." This prevents duplicate time activities in QuickBooks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the integration work with QuickBooks Desktop?">
    No. Beebole integrates with QuickBooks Online only. QuickBooks Desktop is not supported.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know if an export succeeded?">
    After an export, Beebole shows an **Entries successfully exported** count. If any entry failed, an **Entries not exported** list appears that you can expand to see the details. You can also open QuickBooks to verify the time activities were created. If you see **Export stopped: QuickBooks sync had errors** instead, nothing was exported: the structure sync that runs first found problems, listed under **Entries not synced**. Resolve them and export again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are expenses exported to QuickBooks?">
    No. Only time entries are exported, as time activities. Expenses are handled in Beebole only and are not synced to QuickBooks.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
