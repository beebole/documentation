Beebole works on any device with a web browser — phone, tablet, or desktop. You can also install it as an app on your device for a native-like experience, complete with an app icon and full-screen mode.

<Info>
  This page covers installing the Beebole web app on your device (PWA). The separate [Beebole desktop app](/help/documentation/desktop-app) is a companion that drafts time entries from your computer activity — you can use both.
</Info>

---

## Installing Beebole as an app

Beebole is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it directly from your browser without going through an app store. Once installed, Beebole appears as a standalone app on your home screen or taskbar.

### On iPhone or iPad

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Beebole in Safari">
    Navigate to your Beebole account URL in Safari.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the Share button">
    Tap the **Share** icon at the bottom of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Home Screen">
    Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**. Give the app a name and tap **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### On Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Beebole in Chrome">
    Navigate to your Beebole account URL in Chrome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the install prompt">
    Chrome displays an **Install** banner or you can tap the menu (three dots) and select **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm installation">
    Tap **Install**. Beebole appears as an app on your home screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### On desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Beebole in your browser">
    Navigate to your Beebole account URL in Chrome, Edge, or another compatible browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Click the install icon in the address bar, or go to the browser menu and select **Install Beebole**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch from your desktop">
    Beebole now appears in your applications list and can be pinned to your taskbar or dock.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The installed app runs in its own window without browser navigation bars, giving you more screen space for your timesheets and reports.
</Tip>

## Responsive layout

Beebole's interface adapts automatically to the width of your screen. It has two layouts:

- **Desktop** — On screens 1280 pixels wide or more: sidebar navigation, the weekly grid, detailed tables, and side panels.
- **Touch** — On narrower screens, which covers phones and most tablets: a compact layout with the sidebar behind a menu button, the timesheet as a list of days, and bottom sheets instead of pop-ups.

A tablet turned sideways can cross the threshold and switch to the desktop layout.

You do not need to switch between different apps or URLs. The same Beebole account works seamlessly across all devices.

## Theme settings

Beebole supports three theme options to match your visual preference:

| Theme | Behavior |
|-------|-----------|
| **Light** | Standard light background |
| **Dark** | Dark background that reduces eye strain in low-light environments |
| **Auto** | Automatically matches your device's system theme setting |

To change your theme, click the button with your initials at the bottom of the sidebar to open the user menu, then pick **Light**, **Dark**, or **Auto**.

## Logging time on mobile

The mobile timesheet is optimized for touch interaction. It presents your time entries as a scrollable list of days rather than a weekly grid, making it easier to navigate on a small screen.

<Frame caption="The timesheet on a phone: a list of days grouped by week, with the + button at the bottom">
  <img src="/help/images/mobile/mobile-timesheet.webp" alt="Beebole timesheet on a phone showing the current week as a list of days, Monday with three time entries, today highlighted, the Submit button at the top and the floating + button at the bottom right" width="390" />
</Frame>

### How to log hours

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your timesheet">
    Tap the hamburger menu icon in the top-left corner to open the sidebar, then tap **Timesheet**.

    <Frame caption="The sidebar opened from the menu button">
      <img src="/help/images/mobile/mobile-sidebar.webp" alt="Beebole sidebar open on a phone over the timesheet, listing People, Projects, Tags, Planning, Timesheet (selected), Reports, Journal and Assistant, with Jordan Reed at the bottom" width="390" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a time entry">
    Tap the **+** button at the bottom of the screen (floating action button) or the **+** icon next to a specific day. The activity selector opens as a bottom sheet: your recent activities under **Your activities**, and the **Working activity**, **Task**, and **Time off** buttons. **Task** and **Time off** only show when your timesheet settings let you record time on tasks or time off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a project, task, or absence">
    Tap one of your recent activities, or tap **Working activity** and work down from a category to the project, **Task** to pick a task, or **Time off** to pick an absence type. Your choice opens the time entry editor.

    <Frame caption="Working activity, then the Client category: its clients, each opening its projects">
      <img src="/help/images/mobile/mobile-activity-picker.webp" alt="Beebole activity selector bottom sheet on a phone showing the Client category with Back above it and the clients Greenleaf Industries, Quantum Logistics, Brightwave Media, Silverline Retail, Northstar Financial and Acme Corp, each with an arrow" width="390" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your time">
    In the editor bottom sheet, enter the duration or set start and end times (depending on your account configuration). Your entry saves automatically — tap the back arrow to return to the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Editing and deleting entries

- **Tap** a time entry card to open the editor bottom sheet and update the duration, project, or comment.
- **Swipe left** on a time entry to reveal the delete action. Confirm deletion in the dialog that appears.

  Editing and deleting are disabled when the timesheet period is locked (submitted or approved). The swipe-to-delete gesture and tap-to-edit are both blocked until the period is unlocked.

### Using the timer on mobile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the timer">
    On a time entry card, tap the play button on the right side of the card. The timer starts and the entry shows a running red counter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor the timer">
    While the timer is running, a timer bar appears at the top of the header showing the active project and elapsed time.

    <Frame caption="A running timer on a phone: the timer bar at the top and the counter on today's entry">
      ![Beebole timesheet on a phone with the timer bar at the top showing Fleet Tracker, its client path and the elapsed time on a red pause button, and today's Fleet Tracker Development entry card with its running red counter](/help/images/mobile/mobile-timer.webp)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop the timer">
    Tap the red pause button in the timer bar or on the entry card. Beebole saves the elapsed time as the entry's duration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The timer only appears on today's entries and is disabled when the timesheet is locked.
</Tip>

### Accepting suggestions on mobile

[Suggested time entries](/help/documentation/ai#suggested-time-entries) reach the mobile timesheet too. They appear as ghost rows inside each day, next to your real entries, so you see the day Beebole is proposing alongside the one you have already logged. The sparkles button in the header, with the number of suggestions on it, shows or hides them.

<Frame caption="Three suggestions on today, with dashed borders and a check to accept each">
  <img src="/help/images/mobile/mobile-suggestion-row.webp" alt="Beebole timesheet on a phone with three suggested entries on Friday, October 2 drawn as dashed rows (Training 4, Sales 2.5, and Website Redesign Development 1.5), each with a green check, under Thursday's logged Sales and Training entries, and the sparkles button showing 3 in the header" width="390" />
</Frame>

- **Accept it as it stands** — Tap the check on the row. The suggestion becomes a normal time entry on that day.
- **Open it first** — Tap the row itself. It opens in a sheet where you can change the duration, set or clear the start and end times, and point it at another task or working activity. The sheet carries three actions: the bin dismisses the suggestion, the play button starts a timer on it, and the check accepts it.

<Frame caption="A suggestion opened in its sheet, with the bin, play, and check actions">
  <img src="/help/images/mobile/mobile-suggestion-sheet.webp" alt="Beebole suggestion sheet on a phone for Acme Corp: Website Redesign, Development, with a Desktop badge, Friday the 2nd, a duration of 1.5 from 9:00 AM to 10:30 AM, the bin at the bottom left and the play and check buttons at the bottom right" width="390" />
</Frame>

A suggestion that carries no project of its own — one that came from a staffing booking, or one on a task your [timesheet settings](/help/documentation/timesheetSettings) don't let you log to — opens the picker instead of accepting straight away, with the message **Choose the project to log this time on first.** Pick the project and the suggestion accepts normally, keeping its link to the planned task.

<Info>
  Suggestions are private drafts. Nothing reaches your timesheet, or your approver, until you accept it.
</Info>

### Submitting timesheets on mobile

When your timesheet is ready for approval, tap the **Submit** button in the header. The button is visible when the period is in draft or rejected state and you are viewing your own timesheet.

After submission, the period is locked and the header shows the **Submitted** status badge. If your approver rejects the timesheet, the period unlocks and a **Resubmit** button appears in its place.

### Navigating periods with infinite scroll

The mobile timesheet uses infinite scroll rather than week-by-week navigation:

- **Scroll down** to load future periods automatically.
- **Pull down** from the top of the list to load previous periods. A pull-to-refresh indicator shows when you have pulled far enough to trigger the load.
- Month banners appear in the scroll list whenever the date crosses into a new month.
- A **Today** button appears in the footer when today is out of view. Tap it to scroll back to the current day.

### Approving team timesheets on mobile

Managers and administrators see a team button in the header (person group icon) with a badge showing the number of pending approvals.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the approval sheet">
    Tap the team icon in the header. The approval bottom sheet slides up with two tabs: **Pending** and **Team**.

    <Frame caption="The approval sheet on its Pending tab, with each submitted week and who it waits for">
      <img src="/help/images/mobile/mobile-approval-sheet.webp" alt="Beebole approval bottom sheet on a phone with the Pending tab selected showing 30 timesheets, a Search field, Select all and Show all, and one card per submitted week with the person, hours, how long ago it was submitted, a Submitted badge and the project managers it waits for" width="390" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a timesheet">
    Tap a person's entry in the **Pending** tab to view their submitted timesheet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or reject">
    With the submitter's timesheet open, tap **Approve** or **Reject** in the header. Rejection requires a comment.

    <Frame caption="A submitted week opened from the sheet, with Approve and Reject in the header">
      <img src="/help/images/mobile/mobile-approve-reject.webp" alt="Beebole timesheet of Ana Pereira on a phone, her name in a yellow bar at the top, the week of Sep 13 to Sep 19, 2026 marked Submitted with 40 hours, the Approve and Reject buttons in the header, and her entries day by day" width="390" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also select multiple timesheets using checkboxes and bulk-approve or bulk-reject them from the action bar that appears at the bottom of the sheet.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to download Beebole from an app store?">
    No. Beebole is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that you install directly from your browser. There is no app store download required.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can I do in Beebole on mobile?">
    The mobile layout is focused on day-to-day time tracking: logging and editing time entries, running the timer, submitting your timesheet for approval, and (for managers) approving or rejecting team timesheets. More detailed configuration and reporting are best handled on a larger screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Beebole offline?">
    Beebole requires an internet connection to sync your data. However, the installed PWA may cache some interface elements for faster loading when you reconnect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I switch between dark and light mode?">
    Click the button with your initials at the bottom of the sidebar to open the user menu, then pick **Light**, **Dark**, or **Auto**. The **Auto** option automatically follows your device settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can an administrator set the theme for the entire organization?">
    No. Theme selection is a personal preference. Each person chooses their own theme independently.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
