Assignments in Beebole control which items each person can use: the projects and tasks they can log time against, the time off types, expense types, and work schedules available to them, and the custom fields they see. Use them to keep timesheets short and relevant, so each person only sees what applies to them.

<Info>
  Assignments control which items are _available_ to a person. What a person can _do_ (view or edit data) is controlled by their role, covered in [Roles & Permissions](/help/documentation/roles-authorisations).
</Info>

## How access control works

Beebole resolves access from three places:

| Layer | Where it lives | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account-wide defaults | **Show or hide by default** in **Account Settings** | Decides whether each kind of item is available to everyone by default |
| Item-side access | **Who has access?** on a project, time off type, expense type, or custom field | Adds people and tags to a hidden item, or excludes them from a visible one |
| Person-side access | **Show or hide** on a person, tag, or project | The same relationships, edited from the other direction |

The item-side and person-side panels edit the same underlying links — pick whichever direction is more convenient. All of these settings are saved automatically.

## Set the account-wide defaults

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Click the button with your initials at the bottom of the sidebar to open **Settings**, then click **Account Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Show or hide by default">
    Open the **Show or hide by default** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the defaults">
    Check or uncheck each option. Changes are saved automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The Show or hide by default panel in Account Settings">
  <img src="/help/images/assignments/show-hide-by-default.webp" alt="Beebole Show or hide by default panel with a note explaining that disabled options can be enabled per tag, person or project under Show or hide, and checkboxes for Show all time off, Show all expenses, Show all schedules, Show all tasks, Show all custom fields, Show all secondary projects and Show all projects" width="1080" />
</Frame>

| Option | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Show all projects** | Whether every project is available to everyone by default |
| **Show all secondary projects** | Whether secondary projects are available by default |
| **Show all time off** | Whether every time off type is available to everyone by default |
| **Show all schedules** | Whether every work schedule is available to everyone by default |
| **Show all expenses** | Whether every expense type is available to everyone by default |
| **Show all tasks** | Checked, every task is visible to anyone whose role covers **Assigned tasks**. Unchecked, people see only the tasks assigned to them, owned by them, or managed by them. Assigning a task always grants it, in both modes |
| **Show all custom fields** | Whether every custom field is available to everyone by default |

Secondary projects are the projects picked after the first one in a timesheet row that chains several categories, such as the activity picked after a client project in a row set to **Client** then **Activity**. See [Project categories](/help/documentation/timesheetSettings#project-categories) for how those rows are built.

When an option is disabled here, items of that kind are hidden from everyone until you grant access individually — per item under **Who has access?**, or per person or tag under **Show or hide**.

For secondary projects, the account default can also be overridden per entity: on a specific project, person, or tag, choose **All secondary projects available** or **No secondary projects available** instead of following the default, or show and hide individual secondary projects one by one.

<Tip>
  Hold **⌘** and click any checkbox in the **Show or hide by default** panel to apply the same change to all options at once.
</Tip>

## Grant access per item with Who has access?

Each project, time off type, expense type, and custom field has a **Who has access?** panel. On a task, the same list is called **Potential owners**. Here is where to find it:

| Item | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Click **Projects** in the sidebar, select the project, and open **Who has access?** |
| Time off type | Go to **Settings** \> **Time Off**, select the type, and open **Who has access?** |
| Expense type | Go to **Settings** \> **Expense Types**, select the type, and open **Who has access?** |
| Custom field | Go to **Settings** \> **Custom Fields**, select the field, and open **Who has access?** |
| Task | Click **Planning** in the sidebar, select the task, and open **Potential owners** |

The panel lists who is linked to the item. On a project or a time off type, it has two sections:

- **Individually** — add specific people with the **Select person** selector.
- **By tags** — add whole groups with the **Select tag** selector. Everyone tagged with that tag, or any of its descendants, gets access.

The panel adapts to the account-wide default. When the default already makes the item available to everyone, the sections flip into exclusion lists — **Excluded individually** and **Excluded by tags** — so you list the people who should _not_ have it.

<Frame caption="A project available to everyone, so the panel lists exclusions">
  <img src="/help/images/assignments/who-has-access-panel.webp" alt="Who has access? panel on the Acme Corp project with the Available to everyone toggle on, and the Excluded individually and Excluded by tags sections with their Select person and Select tag fields" width="538" />
</Frame>

On a project, the panel also starts with a toggle that overrides the account default for that project: **Available to everyone** or **Unavailable to everyone**. The toggle, and the people and tags you add, cover the project and every subproject below it.

Expense types, custom fields, and tasks name their sections differently:

| Item | Sections | When the item is available to everyone by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expense type | **People**, **Tags**, and **Projects** | **Hide for people**, **Hide for tags**, and **Hide for projects** |
| Custom field | **People**, **Tags**, **Projects**, **Tasks**, and **Time off** | **Excluded individually**, **Excluded by tags**, **Hide projects**, **Hide tasks**, and **Hide time off** |
| Task (**Potential owners**) | **Individually** and **By tag** | No exclusion list: assigning a task always grants it, whether or not **Show all tasks** is checked |

The **Projects** section of an expense type limits it to specific projects. On a custom field, the **Projects**, **Tasks**, and **Time off** sections decide which projects, tasks, and time off types get the field. Which kinds of record can carry a custom field at all is set in its **Custom field visibility** panel. See [Who a field is asked of](/help/documentation/custom-fields#who-a-field-is-asked-of).

<Info>
  Time off types, expense types, and custom fields don't carry their own default toggle — their default comes from the **Show or hide by default** options above. Projects are the exception, with the per-project toggle.
</Info>

Work schedules work differently. A schedule's **Assigned to** panel, in **Settings** \> **Work Schedules**, sets who follows that schedule, not who may use it. Which schedules are available to a person is set only from the person or tag side, under **Show schedules** or **Hide schedules**. See [Assigning a work schedule](/help/documentation/work-schedule#assigning-a-work-schedule).

## Edit access from the person or tag side with Show or hide

The same links can be managed from the people side. Click **People** (or **Tags**) in the sidebar, select a person or tag, and open the **Show or hide** panel. For a person or tag it covers time off, expenses, work schedules, tasks, projects, secondary projects, and custom fields. A project's **Show or hide** panel covers secondary projects, expenses, and custom fields.

Each section follows the account default, just like the item-side panel: **Show projects** lists the projects added for this person, while **Hide projects** lists exclusions when all projects are visible by default. The same pattern applies to **Show time off** / **Hide time off**, **Show expenses** / **Hide expenses**, **Show schedules** / **Hide schedules**, and custom fields. Tasks are the exception: because assigning a task always grants it, the section is always **Show tasks**, whether or not **Show all tasks** is checked.

On a person or tag, the secondary project section appears only when a timesheet row chains several project categories. Archived items, and tasks marked done, drop out of these sections, but their links still apply and show again once the item is unarchived or reopened.

Use this direction when onboarding one person onto several projects at once; use **Who has access?** when staffing one project with several people.

### Grant a whole category at once

In the **Show tasks**, **Show projects**, and **Show secondary project** sections of a person or tag, the selector lists each category as a row of its own. Click the category row to grant the whole category in one go; the chevron next to it still expands the category when you want a single item instead. A project's **Show or hide** panel offers the same for secondary projects.

A category granted as a whole stands for every task, project, or secondary project inside it, including anything you add to that category later. It appears as one badge in the category's color, and its members disappear from the selector since they are already covered. Remove the badge to take the whole category back. When projects or secondary projects are shown to everyone by default, the section reads **Hide projects** or **Hide secondary project** instead, and picking a category there hides the whole category.

For example, pick the **Client** category under **Show projects** to put every client project on a person's timesheet without listing them one by one, or pick a planning under **Show tasks** to make all of its tasks available to that person.

The same one-click pick works in a person's **Manages** panel: adding a project category, a planning, or a tag category such as **Department** makes the person manager of everything inside it, now and in the future. See [People](/help/documentation/people) for the panel itself. Categories are created and renamed from the category menu, described in [Projects](/help/documentation/projects#managing-categories-and-level-names).

## Task assignment

Tasks are independent planning items, separate from projects, and carry their own assignment attributes:

- **Owner**: the single person responsible for the task, with their **% FTE**, the share of their working time planned for it.
- **Potential owners**: the people and tags the task is available to, in an **Individually** section and a **By tag** section. It never turns into an exclusion list, because assigning a task always grants it.

To set either one, click **Planning** in the sidebar, select the task, and open the **Owner** or **Potential owners** panel. See [Task Planning](/help/documentation/planning) for how owners and dates drive the Gantt and Kanban views.

## Which plannings a person can book

Alongside the item-by-item assignments above, one setting decides which whole plannings a person may record time on: **Record time on these plannings**, in the **Categories** tab of the **Timesheet and Planning Settings** panel. Add a planning to the list with **Pick a planning**. See [Timesheet and planning settings](/help/documentation/timesheetSettings#plannings) for the setting itself.

The list cascades like every other timesheet setting. The value on **Account Settings** applies to everyone, a value on a tag overrides it for everyone under that tag, and a value on a person overrides both for that one person — so you can let one team book time on a planning that stays out of everyone else's timesheet.

Beebole enforces the list when an entry is saved, not only in the timesheet's pickers. Creating an entry on a task, or moving an existing entry to another task, checks the task's planning against the list of the person the entry belongs to — not the person doing the typing. An entry that breaks the rule is refused with _Your timesheet settings do not allow recording time on this task._

While the **Allow only planned tasks in the timesheet** restriction is on, the plan replaces this list: every planning counts, and time is accepted only on the tasks the person owns. See [Restrictions](/help/documentation/timesheetSettings#restrictions).

<Info>
  Because the check follows the timesheet's owner, a manager or admin recording time for someone else is held to that person's own list. Entries saved before you changed the list stay as they are and remain editable in every other way.
</Info>

## Example: a client project for a five-person team

Suppose the **Website Redesign** project for **Acme Corp** should only appear on five people's timesheets:

1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar and select the project.
2. Open **Who has access?** and switch the toggle to **Unavailable to everyone**.
3. Under **Individually**, add the five people — or tag them and add the tag under **By tags**.

Only those five now see the project when they log time. Everyone else's project list stays uncluttered.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between assignments and role permissions?">
    Assignments decide which items are available to a person in Beebole — which projects, time off types, and expense types they can pick. Role permissions decide what they can do with data — view it or edit it, and for whom. Both apply at the same time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I make a project visible to only a few people?">
    Open the project's **Who has access?** panel, set the toggle to **Unavailable to everyone**, then add the right people under **Individually** or a tag under **By tags**. Only the people you add see the project in Beebole.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I grant access to a whole department at once?">
    Yes. Tag the department's people, then add that tag under **By tags** in the item's **Who has access?** panel (under **Tags** on an expense type or a custom field). Anyone tagged later inherits the access automatically, including through descendant tags.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I set what everyone sees by default?">
    In Beebole's **Account Settings**, under **Show or hide by default**. Each option — projects, secondary projects, time off, expenses, work schedules, tasks, and custom fields — decides whether that kind of item is available to everyone or hidden until granted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I give someone access to a whole category of projects or tasks?">
    Yes. In the person's or tag's **Show or hide** panel, open the **Show projects** or **Show tasks** selector and click the category row itself, for example **Client**, instead of a single item. Everything in that category is granted, including projects or tasks added later, and the category shows as one badge you can remove at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
