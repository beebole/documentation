<Info>
  Accrual policies are not available yet. This page previews how they will work once they ship. Today, you add earned time by hand in the allowance's **Accrued** field. The [carry-forward rules](#carry-forward-rules) and [accrued balances](#viewing-accrued-balances) described below already work.
</Info>

Beebole's accrual policies will let you define how time off accumulates for your team on a recurring schedule. Instead of granting the full allowance on day one, leave will build up gradually, for example every week or every month. Until then, you record earned time by hand in the allowance's editable **Accrued** field.

---

## How accruals will work

An accrual policy will belong to an absence type and define three things:

1. How much time off is awarded per period (the **Quantity**).
2. How often the accrual is awarded (the **Frequency**).
3. When, within each period, the accrual is credited to the person's balance (the **Awarded on** setting).

Each absence type will have its own policy, and policies will be optional: some types can accrue (vacation, for example) while others keep a fixed allowance. Earned time will be added to the person's balance, alongside the **Accrued** amount you can already enter by hand.

---

## Planned policy settings

The policy is planned as an **Accruals** panel on each absence type, under **Settings** \> **Time Off**, switched on and off with a toggle. Once it is on, it will offer these settings:

| Setting | What it will do |
| --- | --- |
| **Frequency** | How often time is credited. See [Frequency settings](#frequency-settings). |
| **Accruing from first** | For **Weekly** and **Bi-weekly** frequencies only, the weekday each accrual period starts on. |
| **Awarded on** | When within each period the credit lands: **first day of the period**, **last day of the period**, or **day after the period**. See [Award timing explained](#award-timing-explained). |
| **Quantity** | The number of hours or days credited per period, in the absence type's unit. |
| **Allow for future time-off requests against accruals** | Lets people book time off ahead of the accruals that will cover it. |

---

## Frequency settings

The accrual frequency will set the cadence at which time off accrues. The planned options are:

| Frequency | Accrual occurs | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily** | Once per day | Leave that builds up with every calendar day |
| **Weekly** | Once per week | Hourly or shift-based teams that accrue with each pay week |
| **Bi-weekly** | Every two weeks | Organizations on a bi-weekly payroll cycle |
| **Semi-monthly** | Twice per month | Organizations that pay on the 15th and end of month |
| **Monthly** | Once per month | Gradual accumulation throughout the year |

<Info>
  To spread a yearly amount evenly, divide it by the number of periods in a year. For example, 20 days a year accrued monthly is about 1.67 days per month.
</Info>

---

## Award timing explained

The **Awarded on** setting will control the moment within each accrual period when the time is credited:

- **first day of the period**: the time is available as soon as the period starts. For monthly accruals, this means the 1st of each month.
- **last day of the period**: the time is credited on the final day of the period, so the person earns it by the end of the accrual window.
- **day after the period**: the time is credited on the first day of the following period, once the person has fully completed the period.

<Info>
  Award timing decides when a person can use accrued time off. With **day after the period**, January's accrual becomes available on February 1, not during January. Pick **first day of the period** if people should use their time right away.
</Info>

## Carry-forward rules

Carry-forward decides what happens to unused time off when an allowance period ends. It already works today, and it is set on the allowance, not on the accrual policy. Two fields on each allowance control it:

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Valid until** | Unused time carries over only when this date falls after the end of the allowance's period. The carried time then stays usable until that date and expires after it. Without a **Valid until** date, nothing carries over: unused time off expires when the period ends. |
| **Carry forward limit** | The maximum amount that carries over. Anything above the limit expires at the end of the period. A limit of 0 means no cap, so the whole remaining balance carries. |

Once the new period starts, Beebole spends the carried balance first, before the new period's allowance, so the time that expires soonest is used first. A negative balance at the end of a period carries nothing. Any **Accrued** amount is part of the balance, so it carries over under the same rules.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the allowance">
    Open the **Absence allowances** panel (in **Settings** \> **Account Settings** for the organization default, or on a person or tag) and click the relevant allowance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the validity date">
    In **Valid until**, pick the last day the carried time can be used. It must fall after the end of the allowance's period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the limit">
    Enter the maximum amount that can roll over in **Carry forward limit**, or leave it at 0 for no cap. Every change is saved automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With the allowance above, if Elena takes no more PTO in 2026, she ends the year with 8 of her 20 days left: 5 carry into 2027 and 3 expire on December 31. Her next time off in 2027 uses the 5 carried days first, and any still unused on March 31, 2027 expire.

<Warning>
  Beebole works out carried balances from the allowance's current settings. Changing **Valid until** or **Carry forward limit** after a period has ended also changes what carried over from it, and every balance that depends on it. Review the affected balances after such a change.
</Warning>

## Viewing accrued balances

You can check any person's balance, including the **Accrued** amount, from their profile:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person's profile">
    Click **People** in the sidebar and open the person's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the balance">
    In the **Absence allowances** panel, each allowance's summary line shows its **Allowance**, **Accrued**, and **Consumed** amounts. Click an allowance to open all of its fields. An allowance inherited from a tag or the organization is read-only on the person.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Balances update as time-off entries are recorded on timesheets and when the allowance's **Accrued** field is adjusted. For everyone at once, use the [Absence quotas report](/help/documentation/reports#absence-quotas).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are accrual policies available in Beebole today?">
    Not yet. Automatic accrual policies are coming soon to Beebole. Today, each allowance has a manual **Accrued** field: enter the time a person has earned there, and Beebole adds it to their balance. Carry-forward on allowances already works.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I give people different accrued amounts?">
    Yes. In Beebole, the **Accrued** field belongs to each allowance, so you can enter a different amount for each person whose allowance is set on their own profile. Accrual policies, once available, will be set per absence type.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to unused time off at the end of the period?">
    It depends on the allowance. Without a **Valid until** date after the period end, unused time off expires when the period ends. With one, the unused balance carries over until that date, capped by the **Carry forward limit** when it is above 0, and Beebole spends it before the next period's allowance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a Carry forward limit of 0 mean nothing carries over?">
    No. In Beebole, a **Carry forward limit** of 0 means no cap: the whole remaining balance carries over, as long as the allowance has a **Valid until** date after the end of its period. To let nothing carry over, leave **Valid until** empty.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will accruals apply to all absence types?">
    No. Beebole's accrual policies will be optional and set per absence type. You will be able to accrue some types (for example, vacation) and keep a fixed allowance for others (for example, sick leave).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
