Beebole's time-off management lets you define absence types, assign individual allowances, and track balances in real time. Your team records time off directly on their timesheets — each entry counts against the balance as soon as it's recorded — and the approval workflow lets managers review every absence.

<Info>
  Time off in Beebole is tracked through **absence types** — categories like vacation, sick leave, or parental leave that you define to match your organization's policies.
</Info>

## Understanding absence types

An absence type represents a category of leave your organization recognizes. Common examples include vacation, sick leave, parental leave, and personal days — but you can create as many types as you need.

Each absence type can be configured with:

- **A name** — The label your team sees when logging time off (e.g., "Vacation", "Sick Leave").
- **A unit** — Whether absences are tracked in **Hour** or **Day** units.
- **A paid setting** — Whether absences of this type count toward people costs. See [Time off and people costs](#time-off-and-people-costs).
- **An allowance** — The amount of time off each person receives per period.

## Creating absence types

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Time Off settings">
    Go to **Settings** \> **Time Off**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new absence type">
    Click **Add Time Off Type**, enter a **Name** for the absence type (e.g., "Vacation", "Sick Leave"), and click **Add Time Off Type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the unit">
    In the **Units** panel, select whether this absence type is tracked in **Hour** or **Day** units. Below it, **Is paid (included in people costs)** is checked by default — uncheck it for unpaid leave. Changes are saved automatically, and the new absence type is available for allowances and timesheet entry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Create separate absence types for each leave category your company offers. This gives you granular reporting and lets you set different allowance rules per type.

## Archiving absence types

When a leave category is no longer offered, archive its absence type instead of deleting it — past time-off records stay intact while the type disappears from day-to-day use.

1. Go to **Settings** \> **Time Off** and open the absence type.
2. Click the **⋯** action menu next to its name, then **Archive**.

<Frame caption="The action menu next to an absence type's name">
  <img src="/help/images/timeoff/absence-type-action-menu.webp" alt="Beebole PTO absence type panel with its action menu open: Duplicate, Rename, Archive, Unarchive (greyed out) and Delete" width="576" />
</Frame>

To restore an archived type, click **Show Archived** in the list, open the archived type, and click the **⋯** action menu, then **Unarchive**. The same menu also offers **Duplicate**, **Rename**, and **Delete**.

## Setting up allowances

An allowance — Beebole's term for a time-off quota or entitlement — defines how much time off a person receives for a given absence type within a specific period. You can set allowances on the organization for everyone, on a tag for a group, or on an individual person.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Absence allowances panel">
    For an organization-wide default, go to **Settings** \> **Account Settings**. For a group, click **Tags** in the sidebar and open the tag. For one person, click **People** in the sidebar and open their profile. Each shows an **Absence allowances** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the allowance">
    Click **\+ Add new allowance** and select the absence type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the period">
    Set the start and end dates of the allowance period (e.g., January 1 to December 31 for an annual allowance).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the amount">
    Enter the amount the allowance grants. Each field states the unit it is counted in — days or hours, following the absence type's own unit — so an hour-based allowance can't be mistaken for a day-based one. Every change is saved automatically — there is no save button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel lists the allowances that start in the current year or later. Older ones are hidden: click **Show past allowances** (the badge counts them) to see them, and **Hide past allowances** to collapse them again.

<Info>
  A person uses the allowances of the most specific level that has any: their own profile first, then their tags, then the organization. Beebole takes that level's whole set and does not combine levels per absence type. As soon as you add one allowance on a person's profile, that person stops inheriting every allowance from their tags and the organization, and any absence type without an allowance on their profile has no limit for them. To change a single allowance for someone, click **Copy** in the **Absence allowances** panel where the allowances are defined (**Account Settings** or the tag), click **Paste** in the person's panel, then adjust the one that differs. See [Attribute copy and paste](/help/documentation/concepts#attribute-copy-and-paste).
</Info>

### Carrying unused time off forward

Unused allowance carries into the next period only when the allowance has a **Valid until** date after the end of its period. Without one, nothing carries over, and whatever is left expires when the period ends. Two fields on the allowance control carry-forward:

- **Valid until**: the last day the carried balance can be used. Carried time still unused after that date expires.
- **Carry forward limit**: the maximum amount that carries over. Anything above the limit expires at the end of the period. Leave it at 0 for no cap, so the whole remaining balance carries.

Once the new period starts, Beebole spends the carried balance first, before the new period's allowance, so the time that expires soonest is used first. A negative balance at the end of a period carries nothing.

For example, take a 20-day allowance for 2026 with a **Carry forward limit** of 5 days and **Valid until** set to March 31, 2027. A person who took 12 days in 2026 has 8 days left: 5 carry into 2027 and 3 expire on December 31. Their next time off in 2027 uses those 5 carried days first, and any of them still unused on March 31 expire.

The carried balance counts everywhere the allowance does: booking limits respect it, and the [Absence quotas report](/help/documentation/reports#absence-quotas) shows it per person, including the **Valid until** and **Carry forward limit** columns.

## Tracking balances

Beebole tracks time-off balances from the allowances, their manual **Accrued** amounts, and the absences recorded on timesheets. **Allowance**, **Consumed**, and **Accrued** sit at the top of each card in the **Absence allowances** panel, right after the absence type, and every figure carries its unit — days or hours — in the input and in the card's summary line:

| Balance field | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Allowance** | The amount of time off granted for the allowance period |
| **Consumed** | The time off already recorded over the period (shown on a person's allowances) |
| **Accrued** | Extra time you add to the allowance by hand, for example time earned during the period. Beebole does not calculate it: automatic accrual policies are not available yet. |

The **Carry forward limit** states its unit the same way. Balances update as team members log time off on their timesheets and when you adjust the **Accrued** field on the allowance.

## Negative balance controls

By default, Beebole prevents people from booking more time off than their available balance. You can change this behavior per allowance.

To let a balance go below zero — useful for organizations that handle overdrawn leave manually or at year-end — open the allowance in the **Absence allowances** panel and check **Allow negative balance**. The change is saved automatically. When the box is unchecked, Beebole blocks time-off entries that would push the balance below zero.

<Warning>
  If you allow negative balances, monitor them regularly, for example in the [Absence quotas report](/help/documentation/reports#absence-quotas).
</Warning>

## Time off and people costs

Each absence type decides whether its time off counts as a people cost, through the **Is paid (included in people costs)** checkbox. It sits in the type's **Units** panel, right below the unit, and it is checked on every new absence type. To change it, go to **Settings** \> **Time Off**, open the absence type, and check or uncheck the box. The change is saved automatically.

| Is paid (included in people costs) | What happens in cost figures |
| --- | --- |
| Checked | Each absence is valued with the person's [cost rate](/help/documentation/costs) for that day — Beebole multiplies the absence duration by the rate exactly as it would for a regular time entry — so paid leave appears in the people cost totals of your reports, even though no project time was tracked. Time off is never recorded on a project, so it does not draw on a project's budget. |
| Unchecked | Absences of this type carry no cost. Their hours still show in reports, but they add nothing to cost totals. Use this for unpaid leave. |

<Info>
  Reports apply the checkbox when they run, so changing it also changes the cost of time off recorded before the change. Time off never affects billing amounts, whichever way the box is set — only costs.
</Info>

## Absence approval

Time-off entries follow the same approval workflow as regular time entries. When a person records an absence on their timesheet and submits it, the assigned manager reviews and approves or rejects it.

Approval does not gate the balance: every recorded time-off entry counts against the allowance immediately, even before it is reviewed. Approving locks the timesheet; if an absence is rejected, the person can edit or remove the entry, which restores the balance.

<Info>
  The approval workflow for absences is part of Beebole's general timesheet approval process. See [Approvals](/help/documentation/approval) for details on configuring approval stages and managers.
</Info>

## Recording time off on the timesheet

Team members record absences directly on their timesheets, just like regular time entries, in either the calendar view or the grid view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the timesheet">
    Click **Timesheet** in the sidebar and go to the relevant period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the absence">
    In the calendar view, drag across an empty time slot and pick the absence type (e.g., "Vacation") in the activity picker. In the grid view, click the **+** button (**Add a row**) of the **Time Off** section and pick the absence type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the time off">
    In the grid view, enter the hours on each day of the row for an hour-based type. For a day-based type, each day offers two buttons instead: ½ (**Half day**) and 1 (**Full day**). Click the selected button again to clear the day. In the calendar view, the entry covers the slot you selected: drag or resize the block to change it. A day-based block snaps to a half or a full day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the timesheet">
    Click **Submit** to send the timesheet for approval. The time off counts against the balance as soon as it is recorded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Two days of PTO on a PTO row, under the Time Off section of the grid">
  ![Beebole timesheet grid view for the week of October 4 to 10, 2026, with a PTO row in days under the Time Off section: Monday and Tuesday set to 1 day, the half-day and full-day buttons on the other weekdays, and those two days shaded as days off](/help/images/timesheets/time-off-row.webp)
</Frame>

<Info>
  Day-based time off follows the person's [work schedule](/help/documentation/work-schedule): a **Full day** equals the hours scheduled that day, and a **Half day** half of them. Beebole refuses any other amount. Day-based time off also can't be booked on a public holiday or on a scheduled day off, since those days have no working hours to take off. Hour-based time off is not affected by these two rules.
</Info>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I set different time-off allowances for different people?">
    Yes. Add allowances in the **Absence allowances** panel of a person's profile under **People**, or on a tag for a whole group. Beebole uses the whole set of allowances from the most specific level that has any: the person, then their tags, then the organization. Once a person has one allowance of their own, they no longer inherit any allowance from their tags or the organization, so copy the inherited allowances onto the person first and then adjust the one that differs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when someone exceeds their time-off balance?">
    It depends on the allowance. If **Allow negative balance** is checked, the person can continue booking time off and their balance shows a negative number. If not, Beebole blocks the entry. A person with no allowance at all for that absence type is never blocked: with no balance to check, Beebole accepts the entry, so add an allowance whenever you want a cap or a balance to show.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does time off interact with approvals?">
    Time-off entries follow the same approval workflow as regular timesheet entries. Absences count against a person's allowance as soon as they are recorded — approval reviews and locks the timesheet but does not change the balance. See [Approvals](/help/documentation/approval).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I track time off in both hours and days?">
    Each absence type uses a single unit — **Hour** or **Day**. If you need both, create separate absence types (e.g., "Vacation (days)" and "Medical appointments (hours)").
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Beebole accrue time off automatically?">
    Not yet. Automatic accrual policies are coming soon to Beebole, and the [Accruals](/help/documentation/accruals) page previews how they will work. Today, each allowance has a manual **Accrued** field: enter the time a person has earned there, and Beebole adds it to their balance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I book a day off on a public holiday?">
    In Beebole, day-based time off is a share of the person's scheduled working day, and a public holiday has no working hours to take off. In the grid view, a day-based row shows no **Half day** or **Full day** buttons on that day, and any other attempt is refused with the message "You cannot record time on a public holiday." The same goes for a scheduled day off in the person's work schedule, such as a weekend. Public holidays never count against an allowance, so there is nothing to book for them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I find absence quotas in Beebole?">
    Under the name **allowances**. What other tools call an absence quota or leave entitlement is an allowance in Beebole: open **Account Settings**, a tag, or a person's profile and look for the **Absence allowances** panel. The consolidated view across everyone is the **Absence quotas** entry of the Reports menu — see [Reports](/help/documentation/reports#absence-quotas).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
