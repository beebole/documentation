The Journal is Beebole's built-in messaging and activity feed. The **Journal** page gathers the messages that concern you and your timesheet approval activity, and every person, project, task, and tag has a Journal of its own where your team discusses that item. To open the page, click **Journal** in the sidebar.

---

## Activity feed

The **Journal** page in the sidebar lists, newest first:

- The messages you wrote, the messages that mention you, and the messages posted on your own Journal.
- Timesheet approval activity. Approvers see each timesheet submitted to them. When a timesheet is fully approved or rejected, Beebole posts a message to its owner, with the reason for a rejection.
- Timesheet reminders.

Approvers also see a banner at the top of the page with the number of timesheets waiting for them, and can approve or reject without leaving the page. See [Approvals](/help/documentation/approval#from-the-journal).

A **New messages** line separates what arrived since your last visit from older items. The **Journal** item in the sidebar shows a badge with your unread mentions plus the timesheets waiting for your approval.

### An item's own Journal

Each person, project, task, and tag has its own Journal. Open the item and click **Show journal**, the button at the top right of its panel, to see the messages posted on it. Click **Show attributes** to return to its settings.

Administrators also see the item's change history there: who changed what, and when, over the last 7 days. These change entries appear only in the item's own Journal, never on the **Journal** page in the sidebar. See [Audit trail](/help/documentation/audit-trail).

Opened from the Timesheet page, a person's Journal also lists their timesheet approval events: submitted, approved, and rejected, with the approval stage and the reason for a rejection.

## Messages and threads

The Journal supports threaded conversations:

- Post messages to share updates, ask questions, or reach your team. A message posted in an item's Journal stays attached to that item.
- Reply to a message to keep the conversation together.
- Format messages with bold, italic, links, and lists.
- Add images, videos, and PDF files to a message, up to 25 MB each. Click the attach button in the toolbar (**Attach a file**), drag the file into the editor, or paste it.
- Use @mentions to reference people, projects, and other items. A mention notifies the person, or the people who manage or are assigned to the item.

Point at a message to show its buttons. Click the reply button (**Reply**) to answer it: the editor opens with **Replying to** and the original author, and your reply shows the message it answers. On a message you wrote, the edit button (**Edit**) and the trash button (**Delete**) also appear, for 30 minutes after you post it. These buttons show only when your role can manage **Journal** messages.

## Filtering the feed

On the **Journal** page, click **Filters** at the top to narrow the feed. You can filter by **Person**, **Project**, **Person tag**, **Project tag**, or **Task tag**, each with **is** or **is not**, or search the text of messages with **Message** and **contains**.

In an item's own Journal, you can narrow the feed to one kind of event instead. Click an event, such as an approval or a change entry, to show only events of that kind alongside the messages, and click it again to show everything. When nothing matches, the feed reads **No entries match this filter**.

When you open a person's Journal from the Timesheet page, the feed shows only their timesheet activity, and **Showing timesheet entries only. Click to show all.** appears above it. Click it to show every event again.

## Notifications in the Journal

Several of your [notifications](/help/documentation/notifications) also land on the **Journal** page: timesheets submitted for your approval, messages that mention you, and timesheet reminders. They show up in the feed next to regular messages, so you have one place to catch up.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the Journal for project-specific discussions?">
    Yes. Open the project in Beebole, click **Show journal**, and post your message there. It stays attached to the project, so everyone who opens the project's Journal finds the discussion in one place. The same works for people, tasks, and tags.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see Journal messages?">
    Beebole's **Journal** page shows you the messages you wrote, the ones that mention you, and the ones posted on your own Journal. A message posted on a project, task, tag, or person appears in that item's Journal. Access depends on the **Journal** permission of your [role](/help/documentation/roles-authorisations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reply to Journal messages from email?">
    Yes. When you receive an email notification about a Journal message, you can reply directly from your email client. Beebole processes the reply and adds it to the thread.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit or delete a Journal message?">
    Yes, for messages you wrote, during the first 30 minutes after posting. Point at the message in Beebole's Journal and click **Edit** or **Delete**. After 30 minutes the buttons no longer appear on that message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Journal show approval activity?">
    Yes. On Beebole's **Journal** page, approvers see the timesheets submitted to them and a banner with the number waiting, and people receive a message when their timesheet is fully approved or rejected. A person's Journal opened from the Timesheet page lists every submission, approval, and rejection. See [Approvals](/help/documentation/approval) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
