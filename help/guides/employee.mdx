Beebole is where you log the hours you work and the time you take off. As an employee, you do all of this on the **Timesheet**, then submit it for approval. This guide walks through those tasks in the order you meet them.

<Info>
  Your administrator sets up Beebole — projects, time-off types, and approval rules are already in place. You sign in with a one-time 6-digit code emailed to you, a passkey, your Google or Microsoft account, or your company's single sign-on. There are no passwords to remember.
</Info>

## Log your time

Open **Timesheet** in the sidebar to record your hours. The timesheet has two views of the same entries. Switch between them with the **Calendar view** and **Grid view** buttons next to the title. An entry you add in one view shows up in the other.

- In the calendar view, each day is a column. Drag across a time slot, or click one, then pick the project or task. Each entry is a block you can move or resize.
- In the grid view, each row is a project or task and each column is a day. Add a row for what you worked on, then type your hours in the day's cell.

Beebole saves your entries as you go. If your administrator turned on the timer, click **Start timer** to time your work live instead of typing hours, then **Pause timer** to save the elapsed time. You can also copy a previous period to reuse the same work.

See [Timesheets](/help/documentation/timesheets) for both views, the timer, and copying a period.

## Record time off

To log vacation, sick leave, or any other absence, record it on your timesheet like other time. In the calendar view, drag across a slot and pick the time-off type. In the grid view, add a time-off row for that type and enter the time on each day. It counts against your allowance as soon as you record it. Your time off is then reviewed with the rest of your timesheet when you submit it, through the same approval workflow.

Each allowance in the **Absence allowances** panel shows its **Allowance**, any **Accrued** adjustment, and how much you have **Consumed**. Remaining and taken figures across people are in the [Absence quotas](/help/documentation/reports#absence-quotas) report. Learn more in [Time off](/help/documentation/timeoff).

## Submit your timesheet

When your period is complete, click **Submit** at the top of the timesheet. This locks the period and sends it into your organization's approval workflow. If **Submit** is missing on a period you haven't submitted yet, no approval workflow applies to you, so there is nothing to submit. Ask your administrator if you think there should be one.

An approver then reviews it and either approves it or rejects it. If it is **Rejected**, a comment tells you why — fix the entries and click **Resubmit**. Once every stage approves, the timesheet is **Approved** and stays locked.

See [Approval](/help/documentation/approval) for how the review process works stage by stage.

## Track time on mobile

Beebole works in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen like an app. From your phone, you can log time, run the timer, record time off, and submit your timesheet. It is the same Beebole account, so your entries are the same on every device.

See [Mobile](/help/documentation/mobile) to install the app and get started.
