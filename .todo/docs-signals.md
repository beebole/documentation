- [ ] HIGH | `help/documentation/approval.mdx` | In "Force approve and reject" and in the FAQ "How do I reopen an approved timesheet so its owner can edit it?", add the exception: a period Beebole approved automatically has no **Reject**, for anyone, administrators included, because no submission exists to send back. That covers periods closed by **Auto-submit timesheets after X days** for a person with no approval workflow, periods submitted while no stage had an approver, and periods people submitted before 2026-10-08 while no workflow applied to them. Adding a workflow afterwards does not make them rejectable. Such a period shows **Auto-approved** in the Journal, and an administrator corrects it with the edit button (**Edit timesheet**) instead. Verified on prod: `frontend/src/components/timesheet/approval-actions.ts` `canRejectApproved` returns false when the state has no stage details ("No stages → auto-approved (no flow). Backend has no submit event to reject against."), `backend/src/lib/approval.ts` gives a standalone auto-approval an empty `stageDetails`, and `rejectTimesheetCore` in `approvalEvent.ts` needs a submit event (`SubmitEventNotFound`). This is the piece left after this release's no-workflow rewrite of the page (gaps.md "Multi-stage approval"), and it is distinct from the recall FAQ `/mine-conversations` proposed for `timesheets.mdx` on 2026-10-09. — evidence: Intercom: 1 conversation (2026-10-06 to 2026-10-08), an administrator whose three colleagues clicked **Submit** with no workflow in place added an **Admins** stage and looked for **Reject** on the approved periods, as the FAQ says, and found none; support confirmed they cannot be reopened. The FAQ states the opposite for this case.
- [ ] MEDIUM | `help/documentation/costs.mdx` (FAQ) | Add "Why doesn't a report use the cost rate set on the person?": Beebole looks for a cost rate on the project first, from the deepest subproject up, and uses the person's rate only when no project rate applies. A rate the project inherits from a project tag or from the organization counts as the project's rate, so a cost rate set on the organization or on a project wins over every person's own rate. To vary the cost per person on a project, use **Split by persons** on that project's rate; to rely on person rates, remove the project or organization rate. Verified on prod: `backend/src/application/reporting/engine/helpers.ts` `findRate` checks projects, then the person, with the comment "No need to check on organisation because it will already be present on either projects or person". The order is stated in "Which rate applies to a time entry", not its consequence; `billing.mdx` already has the matching FAQ for billing rates. — evidence: Intercom: 1 conversation (2026-10-07), costs in an invoicing report did not match a consultant's rate because a test cost rate sat on the project; plus an older thread (2026-09-02, back in the window through a status change) where an organization-wide cost rate hid every person's rate.
- [ ] MEDIUM | `help/documentation/people.mdx` | In "Archiving and offboarding", replace the Warning "Do not delete a person if you want to keep their historical time data" with what happens: Beebole refuses to delete a person who has time or expense records, approval history, tasks they own or people they manage, and names what blocks it (link `/help/documentation/concepts#delete-protection`). Archive them instead, which frees their seat and keeps their records; **Delete** is for a person added by mistake who has no history. Same nuance on the bulk **Delete** bullet. Verified: labels `error.hasTimeRecords`, `error.hasExpenseRecords`, `error.hasApprovalEvents`, `error.isOwnerOf`, `error.isManagerOf`, and the Delete protection table in `concepts.mdx`. — evidence: Intercom: 1 conversation (2026-10-06, Spanish), an administrator who had just moved from Legacy could not delete people migrated as client contacts and asked why; support answered to archive them. The warning implies the deletion would go through and erase their history.
- [ ] MEDIUM | `help/documentation/custom-fields.mdx` | "Entering values" says a field visible for a type "appears automatically on every matching record — there is nothing to add one by one", which holds only while **Show all custom fields** is on in **Show or hide by default** (on for new accounts, `defaultAvailability()` in `backend/src/lib/entity.ts`). Qualify it: with that option off, the field appears only on the people, tags, projects, tasks and time off types listed in its **Who has access?** panel (link `#who-a-field-is-asked-of`). Add a FAQ "Why doesn't my custom field appear on a person?" (check **Visible for People**, then **Who has access?** or **Show all custom fields**) and the keywords `assign custom field to people` and `custom field not showing`. The matching paragraph on `people.mdx` is fixed in this release (gaps.md "Custom fields on persons"); `custom-fields.mdx` still says the opposite. — evidence: Intercom: 1 conversation (2026-10-06), an administrator had defined custom fields and asked how to assign them to people, from a person's profile.
- [ ] LOW | `help/documentation/custom-reports.mdx` | Under "Time and amounts", next to the **Task** row (**Planned**), say that a **Task** column adds a row for every task in the report's period and filters, with its planned effort, whether or not anyone logged time on it, so rows with no hours or cost appear among the time rows; leave the Task column out of a time-only report.
